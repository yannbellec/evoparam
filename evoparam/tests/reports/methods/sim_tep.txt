Evoked responses were analysed with evoparam (version {version}) using the preset 'tep' v1.0; validation: ds002094: REQ-275 not met (N45 and N100 on the channel of maximal response for 9 of 20 subjects with the preset's cleaning, 6 of 20 with ICA cleaning added); late peaks (N100, P180) may be contaminated by auditory and somatosensory responses to the coil click and scalp sensation. The stimulation artifact was removed by interpolation across the artifact window (evoparam.artifact.remove_artifact, method 'interpolate') in the window -2 to 12 ms (linear interpolation). A quality score between 0 and 1 was computed for every trial. The stimulation artifact was removed by subspace projection (evoparam.artifact.remove_artifact, method 'subspace') in the window -2 to 12 ms. A quality score between 0 and 1 was computed for every trial. Channels were re-referenced to the common average. Each trial and channel was baseline-corrected by subtracting the mean of -800 to -100 ms of its own trial. Trials were averaged per channel (mean). In the window 12 to 300 ms, each waveform was modelled as a double exponential background plus up to 8 components (gaussian and skewed gaussian; full width at half maximum 5 to 150 ms, both polarities). Amplitudes and background coefficients were solved by variable projection and the other parameters by bounded least squares (linear loss, 3 starts). Components were added one at a time and kept when the BIC decreased by at least 10, computed on residuals whitened by an autoregressive model of the noise of the baseline. A final model holding two overlapping components of opposite sign was refitted from starts on the extrema of the data and replaced when the refit was better; a component of the refitted model that no longer lowered the criterion by the same amount was removed. Uncertainty: Jacobian-based (Wald) intervals at 95 %. Onset and offset are the times where a component reaches 10 % of its peak amplitude. Components were labelled by latency window: N15 (10 to 20 ms, negative), P30 (22 to 38 ms, positive), N45 (38 to 55 ms, negative), P60 (50 to 75 ms, positive), N100 (80 to 140 ms, negative) and P180 (150 to 250 ms, positive). Classical measures (peak, mean amplitude, area and fractional-area latencies) were computed on the same window. The presence of a response in 12.2 to 300 ms was tested on every channel with a sign-flip permutation test of the trials (max_t statistic, 1000 permutations) against the baseline -800 to -100 ms (both polarities, alpha = 0.05, Benjamini-Hochberg false-discovery-rate correction across channels). Random draws used the seed 0; the result can be regenerated from its recorded provenance with evoparam.reproduce.
