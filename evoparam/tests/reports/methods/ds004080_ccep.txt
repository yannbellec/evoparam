Evoked responses were analysed with evoparam (version {version}) using the preset 'ccep' v1.2; validation: ds004080 (REQ-113, REQ-114, REQ-135). The stimulation artifact was removed by template subtraction (evoparam.artifact.remove_artifact, method 'template') in the window -2 to 8 ms (global template; templates grouped by the metadata columns stim_site, stim_current_mA and stim_polarity (those present); scaled to each trial and channel; recovery tail left to the response model and fitted jointly with its background). A quality score between 0 and 1 was computed for every trial. Channels were re-referenced to an adjusted common average from which channels with a response were excluded (responses judged in 10 to 300 ms). Each trial and channel was baseline-corrected by subtracting the mean of -500 to -20 ms of its own trial. Trials were averaged per channel (mean). In the window 9 to 500 ms, each waveform was modelled as an exponential background plus up to 6 components (skewed gaussian and gaussian; full width at half maximum 3 to 250 ms, both polarities). Amplitudes and background coefficients were solved by variable projection and the other parameters by bounded least squares (linear loss, 3 starts). Components were added one at a time and kept when the BIC decreased by at least 10, computed on residuals whitened by an autoregressive model of the noise of the baseline. A final model holding two overlapping components of opposite sign was refitted from starts on the extrema of the data and replaced when the refit was better; a component of the refitted model that no longer lowered the criterion by the same amount was removed. Uncertainty: Jacobian-based (Wald) intervals at 95 %. Onset and offset are the times where a component reaches 10 % of its peak amplitude. Components were labelled by latency window: N1 (9 to 50 ms, negative), N2 (50 to 300 ms, negative) and delayed (100 to 1000 ms, either polarity). A response was said to have an N1 when the fitted curve (background and components) had a negative peak in 9 to 50 ms deeper than 3.4 times the noise standard deviation estimated by the fit. Classical measures (peak, mean amplitude, area and fractional-area latencies) were computed on the same window. The presence of a response in 9 to 500 ms was tested on every channel with a sign-flip permutation test of the trials (max_t statistic, 1000 permutations) against the baseline -500 to -20 ms (both polarities, alpha = 0.05, Benjamini-Hochberg false-discovery-rate correction across channels). Single-trial parameters were estimated by refitting the channel's components to every trial with the 'shift_scale' trial model (automatic maximum shift); trials whose fit explained less than 20 % of the variance were marked as untrusted. Random draws used the seed 0; the result can be regenerated from its recorded provenance with evoparam.reproduce.
