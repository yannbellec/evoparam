# Code of Conduct

This code of conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
whose full text applies.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the evoparam community a harassment-free experience for
everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Expected: empathy and kindness; respect for differing opinions and experiences; giving and
gracefully accepting constructive feedback; taking responsibility for our mistakes; focusing
on what is best for the community.

Not accepted: sexualised language or imagery and unwelcome advances; trolling, insulting or
derogatory comments, personal or political attacks; public or private harassment;
publishing others' private information without their explicit permission; other conduct
that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all community spaces (repository, issues, discussions, forums) and
when an individual officially represents the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the project author, Yann V. Bellec, who is responsible for
enforcement during 0.x (see [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)), through the contact given on
the author's GitHub profile (https://github.com/yannbellec) or GitHub's private reporting
tools. Every complaint is reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the
reporter are respected. From 1.0 the steering group owns enforcement.

## Enforcement guidelines

Following the Contributor Covenant 2.1 ladder: (1) **correction**, a private written
warning; (2) **warning**, with consequences for continued behaviour and no interaction
with the people involved for a period; (3) **temporary ban** from community interaction;
(4) **permanent ban** for a pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression towards a
person or class of persons.
